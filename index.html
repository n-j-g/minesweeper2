This game

Controls

A tap uses the selected tool. A long-press does the other one: it flags while you're digging and digs while you're flagging. Tap a number whose mines are all marked to open its other neighbors. Use − and + to resize the cells; the board scrolls.

Marks on the board

  • Your flag. The solver ignores it.
  • Placed by the naive fill. Certain.
  • A mine found by Settle.
  • Guaranteed safe, shown by a cheat.
  • Selected, or suggested for Settle.

What the solver does

Naive fill. A number touching exactly as many covered cells as its value gets those cells marked. A number whose marks already match its value opens its other neighbors. This repeats until neither applies. It never combines two numbers, so every overlap puzzle is left for you.

Settle. When nothing on the board is guaranteed safe, the game picks something to settle and scrolls to it: a true 50/50 group if one exists, otherwise the edge cell least likely to be a mine. Settle reveals it with no risk. You can also select a group yourself with the 50/50 tool; it qualifies only if the board allows exactly two layouts for it and nothing could ever tell them apart.
